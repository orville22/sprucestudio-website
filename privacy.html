Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Spruce Studio respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you contact us and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use our form or email us, we collect your website address, email address and anything else you choose to tell us.

How we use it

We use it to look at your current website, prepare your free redesign, send it to you and reply to your questions. We don't sell, rent or share your details for anyone else's marketing.

Who else handles it

Our enquiry form is processed by a third-party form service, and our email is handled by our email provider. They store your message only so it can reach us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we're talking with you or working together, and delete them when they're no longer needed. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just email hello@sprucestudio.com.au.
